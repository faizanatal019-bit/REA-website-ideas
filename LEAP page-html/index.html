Language & Exam Access Program

English opens doors. LEAP helps you walk through them.

If you have refugee or refugee-like experience and are building a health career in Australia, English can shape how you study, work, and connect with your community. Formal English tests and preparation can cost hundreds of dollars. LEAP combines free expert preparation with subsidised test access, so cost isn’t what stands between you and your next step.

What’s included

What participants receive

Free English assessment

Complete a free ILSC online English assessment as part of screening.

Six weeks of preparation

Expert-led classes built around health English, delivered online across two evenings a week with ILSC Language Schools.

Subsidised PTE test access

For participants who attend at least 80% of classes. PTE is the test accepted by AHPRA.

A peer support cohort

Learn alongside others on the same pathway. Community and accountability matter as much as the classes.

Who is it for?

Two pathways into LEAP for Health

Pathway 01 · Pre-enrolment

You’re applying for a nursing or midwifery program at an Australian university or TAFE, and you need an English language test result to receive an offer.

Pathway 02 · Post-completion

You’ve finished (or are close to finishing) an AHPRA-regulated health program in Australia, and you need a test result to register and practise.

Eligibility

Am I eligible?

Eligible participants must meet every core requirement and at least one pathway requirement.

Meet all of these

  • Be a refugee or have refugee-like experience and be residing in Australia, including holders of humanitarian, temporary protection, bridging or permanent resident visas
  • Be pursuing a career in an AHPRA-regulated health profession
  • Achieve an ILSC Intermediate 4 (I4) level or higher in the free online English assessment
  • Intend to sit your English language test within 12 months

Meet at least one of these

  • You are applying for a nursing or midwifery program and need a test result for enrolment, or
  • You have completed or are nearing completion of an AHPRA-regulated program and need a test result to register

How it works

Four clear steps

Express interest

Complete the online expression-of-interest form for LEAP for Health.

Assessment

Complete the free ILSC online English assessment and send your results to REA. You need Intermediate 4 (I4) or higher.

Prepare

Attend six weeks of online preparation classes, at least 80% of them.

Sit

Use your subsidised access code to book and sit the PTE test.

Ready to take the next step in your health career?

Complete the online form to register your interest in a 2027 LEAP for Health cohort. Donations help keep LEAP accessible.
